About this site

This site collects guides about Samsung Tizen TVs: rooting, privacy, speed, firmware updates and smart home control. It is written by the people who build Vetro, an open source launcher, debloater and remapper for rooted Samsung TVs.

How the guides are written

Statements about Vetro come from its repository: the README, changelog and test records. Statements about Samsung and other projects come from the pages we link. Each guide says what was checked in its "Updated" line. When something is a single report or a single test run, the guide says so. When we could not confirm a claim, the guide leaves it out or says it is unconfirmed.

We do not publish exploit steps. The root used by Vetro comes from Chris Ritsen's samsung-tv-root research; the guides explain what it is and link to it.

Corrections

Found something wrong or out of date? Open an issue with the page and the correction. Menu names and firmware behavior change, and we would rather fix a page than leave it wrong.

Independence

Vetro is not affiliated with Samsung. Samsung, Tizen, Bixby and SmartThings are trademarks of their owners. Generative AI and coding agents assisted the development of Vetro and the drafting of these guides; the tests, device observations and cited sources define what has been verified.
