# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Velo look (opus6, trial on branch explore/opus6-ui)

- **Banner, Info and the TV guide on one veil from the bottom-left corner.** The channel banner is a compact block in the bottom-left corner (number in a light 128 px weight standing on the programme line, name, logo, the progress line with the times and "N min left" following its head: when the label would touch a time, that time steps aside). Info is the same corner grown up and to the right (title, line, two lines of description and the next programme, clock, date, source, audio), and the guide covers the screen with the same veil: lanes of rounded programme cells, the focused one filled ice with slate text. The veil is one stretched picture (`tuner/res/veil_corner.png`) whose fade is baked so it reads evenly on bright pictures; the texts carry the soft shadow the launcher already uses. Changing between them is one movement (the veil grows, the shared parts move). Tried in the computer preview only, not on a TV.
- **Nothing moves when the programme arrives.** The banner's big number used to sit on the name's line until the programme was read, then drop 49 px onto the programme's line; it now stands on the programme's line from the start, and the line fills in place. The info panel no longer says "No programme information" while it is still reading: its title row stays empty until the TV has answered, and the message (when there really is none) sits on the title's own baseline.
- **Small alignments found by checking every screen in every state.** The channel pad's heading and the typed number sat 3 and 7 px lower than drawn; the Task manager's heading, free memory and sort order were on three baselines 1 and 2 px apart and are now on one. In the TV guide a channel whose programmes cannot be read says "No information" (and is asked again after 20 s) instead of "Loading…" for ever. The guide no longer opens on top of an open info panel when it is asked for from Vetro rather than from the remote.
- **One material for every on-screen display.** The launcher bar, the input page (Source), the Task manager, the channel pad, the channel list and the audio message now use the 123 pad's material: slate at 90% with a thin light edge, no drop shadow, 40 px corners for panels and full capsules for one-row elements; one focus everywhere (an ice capsule with dark text; the app icons keep their ring). The channel pad and the channel list sit at the right, centred like the volume bar at the left; channel numbers in the list are right-aligned. Fixes found on the way: the input page was 34 px left of centre, the task list could show an empty row when two running apps shared an id, and the number typed in the 123 pad is now on a small chip, readable on bright pictures. Tried in the computer preview only.

### Apps that came with the TV: switch them off, or delete them

- **Disable has switches for the apps Samsung ships**: Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, Apple TV, the Internet browser and Gallery (Quick settings → Disable, "Apps you don't use"). A disabled app cannot start, from its tile or from its button on the remote, and it loses its tile on the launcher's row and on All apps; the same switch, or Restore the TV, brings it back. A TV that does not have the app does not show its switch. Not tried on a TV yet for these apps (for Netflix, whether it also stops the app starting by itself in the background is not known).
- **New tab, Uninstall.** A short list of apps that came with the TV and can be deleted for good: the e-Manual (21 to 124 MB on the TVs of the community's reports), Samsung TV Plus, Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV, Disney+, Spotify, Xbox, GeForce NOW, Amazon Luna, the Internet browser, Gallery and Samsung Health, each with its icon and its size. The two tabs say what sets them apart: **Disable** switches an app off and leaves it on the TV, **Uninstall** deletes it and Vetro cannot bring it back (most can be installed again from Samsung's Apps). An app is offered only when the TV has it and its own registry allows removing it, so a TV where Netflix is part of the firmware does not list Netflix; the list is by name because the registry marks the store and the account apps as removable too (57 of 57 TVs in the reports). Each uninstall asks for a confirmation and goes through the action of Apps and processes, one package at a time, with its check that the package is gone, the clean-up of Samsung's Home, Keep removed and the launcher's rebuild. **Keep removed apps removed** moved to this tab. Not tried on a TV yet for these apps.

### Review before the release: problems left behind, stability and speed

- **The dashboard and the TV screen do less for the same picture.** The dashboard asked the TV for 13 readings when it opened and for all eight Quick settings readings each time Quick settings opened, whatever tab was on screen: now 7 at start, each tab reads only what it shows, and nothing read in the last 30 s is asked again (opening Quick settings again within 30 s: 9 requests before, none now). A section is drawn again only when what it shows changed, so a background reading no longer closes an open detail or takes the focus away (78 section redraws at start before, 18 now; a Quick settings switch 18 → 4, a Vetro UI switch 47 → 3), the launcher preview keeps its pictures while its switches redraw, the slideshows and the live countdown stop while their page is not shown, and the tuner's slideshow and the About photo are built when they are opened. On the TV screen an arrow key changes the class of two tiles instead of rebuilding the screen (163 elements replaced per key before, none now), and the PIN slot is not written again when nothing changed. Measured on a copy of the service with the simulated TV and the CPU slowed 4×; not yet on a phone or a TV.
- **TV guide: a "No information" cell is striped from end to end.** The stripes were a rectangle 20 px in from each end, laid over the plain rounded cell: the two ends showed as solid caps without lines, and the middle had the cell's faint white twice. The cell is now only the stripes, with rounded striped ends (`hatch_ends.png`, made by `tuner/tools/make-hatch-ends.py`) that continue the lines of the middle.
- **The launcher keeps its app tiles on firmware without Samsung's "featured" column.** The app list now also reads which apps Samsung's own Apps screen lists, from a column seen only on the Q80A; where a TV's registry lacks it, the whole read failed and every app tile would have gone, with the update then rolled back. The read of 0.5.0 runs instead there.
- **Update Vetro on the TV screen says what to do without root access.** On Tizen 10, Tizen 5.5 and older, or before root connects, it offered an update that could never finish. It now says the new version is out and is installed with Apps2Samsung, and nothing is downloaded.
- **The Remapper lists only the parts of a remote that send keys** ([#217](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/217), [#218](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/pull/218), by swiezaczek). A Bluetooth Smart Control shows up as several devices with the same name, some without keys; they made the name ambiguous and "No key captured" followed.
- **Removing Vetro is never blocked by a restore step** ([#212](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/212)). The Remove section now says that deleting the app on the TV always works; only erasing Vetro's saved data waits for the restore.
- **HbbTV can be turned back on where the TV has no HbbTV service app** ([#212](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/212)). On TVs from 2023 on (24 of the 41 rooted TVs in the community reports) the service app does not exist; turning HbbTV on tried to start it, the TV refused, the whole change was rolled back, and "Remove Vetro's saved data" could not finish. The start is now skipped where there is nothing to start.
- **Home Assistant no longer reconnects every 10 seconds** ([#199](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/199), [#213](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/213)). The broker's log showed two connections from the same TV with the same client id, each taking the session from the other. A second copy of the service (it cannot listen on the port, and since 0.5.0 it waits instead of ending) connected to the broker too. The MQTT connection now belongs to the copy that answers on the port. Not yet confirmed by the reporters.
- **Resume plugins starts them at once** ([#190](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/190)). It only cleared a flag and asked for a restart of Vetro that no screen offers. The paused Shards now start when the button is pressed; if Vetro is stopped twice in a row again right after, they are paused again.
- **MQTT on TVs with Node 4.4** ([#77](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/77)). Building a packet from a list of bytes ended the service on that Node as soon as a broker was configured. Fixed in the compatibility layer; not run on such a TV yet.
- **Switch to an input from a rule made in the dashboard** ([#204](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/204)). The service already accepted `KEY_HDMI1` to `KEY_HDMI4` and `KEY_TV`; the rule editor now offers them.
- **A slow start is no longer "Start failed"** ([#179](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/179)). Right after a boot the start command of the launcher (and of the tuner) gets a long wait, and one that still runs out of time is not a failure when the process comes up from it; the first retry inside the boot window is after 10 s, not 30; one failed status read while waiting no longer fails the start. Other boot tasks (the domain filter, the wins, keep-removed, the Startup cache) wait until the launcher is up, at most a minute, so their long commands do not queue in front of it. `GET /dock` reports `bootTimeline`, the seconds since boot of each step.
- **After Vetro is installed again over a running one, the launcher and the tuner are started again.** They run in units of their own and outlive the service: the old launcher kept running with the new service, without the new options and command words (the volume keys did nothing until a reboot). Each now remembers which build started it. If the TV is restarted before the new Vetro ran, the launcher first starts from the lists the older Vetro wrote (no All apps, tiles that cannot be moved); about five minutes after the boot it is started again once, with lists of its own. Tried on the computer with the files 0.5.0 writes, not on a TV.
- **"Version differs" no longer shows on a TV that updated itself.** The TV screen compared the version of the package with the service's, and a self-update changes only the service: every updated TV was told an old service was still running and to reinstall. It now warns only when the service is older than the package (an old one still running after an install).
- **Updating from 0.5.0, checked end to end on the computer.** The updater of each published 0.5.0 was run against this version's files with a real shell: the result is this version file for file, and a wrong hash, a full disk, a killed shell or a new version that never confirms all put 0.5.0 back. Four things found on the way are fixed:
  - **A port chosen in Apps2Samsung survives an update from the dashboard.** The chosen port is written into the package's files, and an update replaced the service and the TV screen with ones built for 8099: the service moved to the port the user had avoided and the TV screen could not reach it. Both now read the port from the package's `config.xml`, which an update never replaces. Not tried on a TV with a custom port yet; until then, such a TV is better updated with Apps2Samsung (entering the port again).
  - **"Go back to the previous version" is offered only for this version's own update.** The saved files outlive an install: after Vetro was installed again with another version, going back laid an older version's files over the new package.
  - **Go back, then the same update again, restarts the launcher and the tuner.** The state still named this build, so the launcher the older Vetro had started kept running. The process a build started is now remembered with it.
  - **The SDB tunnel comes up after an install over a running Vetro.** Its port was still held by the older service for a few seconds, and the tunnel stayed down until the next restart; it now tries again.
- **The two fixes republished with 0.5.0 are in this version too**: an older Vetro that still holds the port after an update is ended (and only that), and the TV's debug connection is released when the SDB tunnel is idle ([#168](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/168)).
- **Launcher.** Holding OK to move a tile no longer ends the move by itself (the held key's own repeat was read as the OK that ends it). A change to the row rebuilds only the row, not the Sources page and All apps with all their icons, and Back after a move rebuilds nothing. The Control center opens without waiting for the Bluetooth list (it is read just after the cards come in), keeps the Picture mode and Sleep timer rows it already had instead of "Reading…" at every opening, and gives up on a picture-mode read after 3 s instead of 25. A sound output chosen while another device was still connecting is no longer overridden by it; a Bluetooth service that was not ready at boot is asked again. With the custom launcher given up by the supervision, the volume and 123 keys are given back. The launcher calls Vetro on the port the package was built for.
- **The launcher no longer works while nothing moves.** The osd 123 drew the same frame at every screen refresh for as long as it stayed open, and the Volume bar did the same for the whole time it is held: both now wait for the next key or for the fade-out. The Control center no longer measures every text and moves every object of its cards on each frame, only what changed is placed, so moving the selection redraws the capsule and little else. Home draws the Dock's first frame before it starts the shell that keeps Samsung's Home away; a Home pressed while the launcher is still starting opens it as soon as it is ready instead of being lost; the look for new remote nodes every 3 s reads the device list only when /dev/input changed; only the cards chosen in the dashboard are built (and none of the Dock in custom launcher mode). Nothing looks different. With `--perf` the launcher also logs how long each opening takes (Home, Control center, All apps, a row change, the Volume bar, the osd 123), to be measured on the TV.
- **Samsung's Home left idle after a boot is closed on TVs where it is a .NET app.** After a boot the TV starts its own Home, which sits idle behind the launcher; Vetro closes it once the boot has settled. On a Q80A it never did: the check looked for a process named `csfs`, and there the Home runs under the TV's .NET launcher as `DN_sung.tv.csfs`, so Vetro took it for closed (about 90 MB held for three hours, until the next Home press). Both names count now. The check was run on the Q80A with that process's name; a boot with the corrected check has not been tried yet.
- **An update no longer puts the Banner over the programme.** A tuner started again in place of one that was running (after an update from the dashboard, or an install over a running Vetro) took the screen back and showed the Banner by itself, in the middle of whatever was on. That start is now quiet: for its first 15 seconds the Banner comes up only for a key. Turning the tuner on from the dashboard, a boot and a tuner that had stopped show it as before. Run on a Q80A: an update with Live TV on screen started the new tuner quiet, 1.1 s after its start it was back over the picture; the screen itself was not filmed in those seconds.
- **The tuner has no per-part switches any more.** The Live TV tab listed nine switches (banner, channel list, guide, pad, info, audio key, hold to repeat, logos, keys only when in front), made to test the parts one by one. Turning the tuner on now gives all of it; a part left off by a test build comes back on.
- **Tuner.** Back to the channel watched before no longer loses the place in the list (the next CH+ went to channel 1). Schedules stay good for 15 minutes, not 20 seconds, so a channel change no longer reads 25 channels again. The channel list is read again when the tuner comes back after ten minutes, and asked for again when the first read brings nothing. One refused fast channel change no longer makes every later one slow. The clock's offset is read again when the tuner is shown (summer time). The list or the pad asked for from a shortcut closes the guide or Info first. A long description is cut with an ellipsis instead of running off the screen. OK on the channel already on shows its banner without tuning it again. Reduce motion now reaches the tuner. The log keeps the run before it and does not grow for ever.
- **The tuner no longer works for nothing on a key or while it waits.** A guide key no longer cuts every row's three days of programmes again to draw three hours, and no longer measures again a title it has already cut: the cut is remembered, and a text already on its place is not set again (on the Banner and Info too, where every text has the halo: the programme arriving a second time is not set or measured again). The guide's objects are made a few seconds after the channel list arrives, while nothing is on screen, instead of on the first Up. A channel change no longer puts the whole channel list in order to find the 23 neighbours it reads. A transition no longer allocates memory on each frame. While hidden, the look for new remote nodes every 3 s reads the device list only when /dev/input changed (and once a minute anyway), the app in front is asked for its id only when another one comes in front, and the check of the pictures at start no longer leaves 15 hidden pictures behind. Nothing looks different (checked object by object in the preview, during and after every transition, with the programmes known, loading, missing and with long names). With `--perf` the tuner logs each transition's frames, each key until the next frame, each guide redraw and the start, to be measured on the TV.
- **Dashboard.** The page is sent compressed (about 114 KB instead of 440 KB) and is not sent again to a browser that has it. Opening Vetro UI reads the launcher once, not twice; the previews no longer read every icon back at each slide; the pages are not redrawn every 5 s while nothing changes. A switch of Vetro Shards no longer stays greyed out after an action that changed nothing; the Overview's Control center switch brings back the cards that were chosen, not only the Task manager; a read that fails says so instead of "Reading…". The pictures of the TV screens follow the TV's present look.
- **Less work for the TV when idle, at a boot and at a wake.** While the launcher and the tuner run, Vetro checks on each one with a single shell on the TV instead of a script of 10 (launcher) or 12 (tuner) processes, and less often once it has seen them running for a while: every 10 seconds at first, then every 30 seconds, then every minute, never longer, and every 10 seconds again after any change. The full check still runs before a start and when the dashboard asks. After a wake the root agent is launched again within about 2 seconds of the TV's debug service answering, instead of when a retry timer set before standby runs out, and restoring the remote's interception is the first command once the agent is back. A remote interception that keeps failing is tried every 30 seconds after the first minute, not every 5 seconds for ever. At boot Vetro reads its own two startup flags from one row of the app registry instead of the whole registry, skips the DNS filter's read on a TV that never used it, and builds the Startup tab's list after the first five minutes. The custom launcher looks up its app in one row too. JSON answers over 1 KB go to the dashboard compressed (the app list of a Q80A is about a tenth of its size), and app icons are not sent again to a browser that has them. GET /status now shows the root commands of the last ten minutes and how long the last reconnect took, to check this on a TV. Not tried on a TV yet.
- **The dashboard's Task manager page is now Apps and processes.** The name Task manager belongs to the card of the launcher's Control center; the page that lists the TV's apps, components and running processes no longer shares it (the menu, the page title and every sentence that points to it).
- **A denser dashboard with shorter words.** Settings run in two columns where the page is wide, a preview sits beside its settings instead of above them, and each setting says one short sentence (the long explanations are behind a disclosure or gone). Vetro UI's tabs are a third to a half shorter (the Launcher tab was 2,200 px tall at 1280 px and is now two views of about 1,000). The Launcher tab has three parts: **Vetro launcher** with its **Customization** (the app row, HDMI sources, source images, Reduce motion, Fix TizenTube), **Control center** with one switch per card, and **Custom launcher**. On a phone, and on an old TV browser, everything stays in one column.
- **TV screen.** Holding OK on the Update tile no longer finds, confirms and installs in a row; a tile cannot stay on "Working…"; the reason a PIN cannot be shown no longer flickers away; the checks panel no longer covers the header when several explanations are long.
- **Alignment of the Control center** (measured on every card): the name and the value of a row stand on one baseline, a row's text no longer moves 1 px when it takes the focus, the footer is centred in its band, the focus capsule holds its content with the same inset on both sides, the header's second line is cut inside the card ("TV off in 120 min", "Set in Samsung's menu"), the labels of the pills are centred on their capitals, and the tick on a focused tile of All apps no longer touches the next tile.
- **Removed**: the old card material of the tuner (code and four pictures), the leftovers of the Task manager's sort, pictures no screen loads, and the Task manager's older single switch in the service.

### Your own launcher row, and All apps

- **Hold OK on a tile of the launcher to move it or take it out of the row.** Left and Right carry it, Down takes it out, OK keeps it there, Back puts the row back. The row starts as Samsung's Home row and becomes yours: Vetro keeps your order and the tiles you took out, lays them over Samsung's row at every start (a newly installed app still arrives at the end, an uninstalled one leaves quietly), and never writes Samsung's own Home row. Thanks to [arnaudaprahamian](https://github.com/arnaudaprahamian) (#43), [tomcraft](https://github.com/tomcraft) (#152), [dancannon](https://github.com/dancannon) (#57, #80), [pponce](https://github.com/pponce) (#118) and [rawldon](https://github.com/rawldon) (#164), whose requests and pull requests this answers.
- **All apps.** The pill at the end of the row (where Settings was) opens a page of every app: the ones Samsung's own Apps screen lists (no helpers, templates or duplicates), Samsung TV Plus and Live TV, by name, then Settings and the App store as pills. A tick marks the apps that are in your row; holding OK adds an app to the end of the row or takes it out. The Launcher tab of Vetro UI has **Use Samsung's row again**.
- **TV and the HDMI inputs in your row.** On the Sources page, holding OK on TV or an HDMI input adds it to the end of the row, or takes it out if it is there, as on All apps; a tick marks the inputs in your row and a short OK still switches. The input comes back at every start, at its place in your order, whether or not it is on Samsung's Home row. Tried in the computer preview only, not on a TV.
- A tap on OK now opens a tile when the key comes up (so that holding it can do something else). Tried in the computer preview only; holding OK on a physical remote has not been tried on a TV yet.

### Control center

- **Down on the launcher's apps row opens the Control center: up to three cards side by side.** It takes the place of the Task manager (by [zabique](https://github.com/zabique), [#172](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/pull/172)), which becomes one of its cards, and adds the **Audio output** card: the TV speakers and the paired Bluetooth audio devices (the connected one on top, the one that plays ticked). OK sends the sound there with the call Samsung's own Sound output list makes, or connects a paired device first; the row says “Switching…” or “Connecting…” and the list is read again a moment later. Left and Right move between the cards (the Task manager's sort keys are gone: the biggest app is always on top), Up on a card's first row or Back returns. On Vetro UI's Launcher tab every card has its own switch and is off until you turn it on; one card keeps the Task manager's width, two or three stand side by side, narrower, as tall as each other (a narrow Task manager leaves out the CPU column), and the hint under the bar reads “Control center”. Whoever had the Task manager on finds it as the only card. Two more cards: **Picture mode** (the modes the TV offers on what is on screen, in Samsung's order; OK switches and saves it for that source; locked while Game mode is on) and **Sleep timer** (Samsung's own timer: off, 30, 60, 120 or 180 minutes, with the minutes left). They read and set Samsung's own settings (the AV daemon over D-Bus, the timer's two system keys) away from the launcher's drawing. Tried in the computer preview only, not on a TV; the Bluetooth calls (`bt_adapter_foreach_bonded_device`, `bt_audio_connect`), the sound output switch, and setting the picture mode or the sleep timer have never run on one (the picture modes and the timer were read on a Q80A).

### Shards

- **A Shard can take over an app's launcher tile.** New optional manifest key `intercepts: [{ "appId", "label" }]` (up to three apps, checked by the loader, the installer and `vetro-plugin.cjs`; not a permission, so older Vetro versions ignore it). While such a Shard is on, pressing that app's tile in the Vetro launcher starts the Shard's `launch(appId)` instead of the app, and the app is started the normal way whenever the Shard is off, declines, fails or is too slow. The launcher list only carries a flag (a seventh column, `shard`); the launcher asks the Vetro service on the TV (`POST /plugins/launcher/api/open`), which asks the Shard, so the launcher never runs anything a Shard wrote. The dashboard shows **Intercepts the YouTube app** (with the app id and what changes) on the Shard's card, in the catalog and in the install question, refuses an install whose bundle intercepts other apps than the catalog entry says, and marks a **Conflict** when two Shards that are on claim the same app (then neither is used). The launcher list is rebuilt when such a Shard starts or stops. Guide: [plugins.md](app/docs/plugins.md#taking-over-an-apps-launcher-tile-intercepts). Needs the new launcher build. Tried on a Q80A with the Ad-free YouTube Shard 0.4.0: the launcher's YouTube tile starts the patched YouTube, with sound; the catalog has the Shard signed with the new key.
- **Samsung's YouTube stays in the launcher when its wrapper app is gone.** When the Home row still lists Samsung's YouTube but its registry row and the wrapper app (`9Ur5IzDKqV.TizenYouTube`) are not on the TV, the tile now starts the Cobalt YouTube app (`com.samsung.tv.cobalt-yt`), and Rebuild the list no longer removes that Home line as a leftover. Tried on a Q80A.

### Fixes

- **Shards are no longer paused because the TV was turned off once.** Vetro put every Shard in safe mode when it had been stopped less than a minute after it started, and the TV being turned off right after an update or a restart counted as that, so the Ad-free YouTube Shard stayed paused and the launcher's YouTube tile opened Samsung's YouTube. Safe mode now needs two such starts in a row; a plugin that keeps crashing Vetro is still caught. Seen on a Q80A.
- **Play / pause on live TV opens the tuner's info panel.** The Smart Control has no Info key; its play / pause key (code 244, Samsung's PlayBack), and the Play, Pause and Play/Pause codes of other remotes (200, 201, 520), now do what Info does, and Samsung's own Info code (188) works too.
- **The launcher's Live TV tile switches to the TV input.** It only brought Samsung's TV app to the front, so after an app (Netflix, a film) the input stayed where the app had left it and Vetro TV Tuner, which covers the TV input only, did not come back. It now switches to the TV input, as the TV entry of the Sources page does. Seen on a Q80A.
- **Samsung Home opens from the launcher on Tizen 9.** On a Samsung S95C and a Q70C (both Tizen 9) the Samsung Home tile closed the launcher and showed nothing: `app_launcher` starts the CoBA Home (`com.samsung.tv.coba.newhome`), reports success and shows no Home, so the second try with First Screen (`com.samsung.tv.csfs`), which only ran after an error, never ran. When the tile is the CoBA Home with First Screen behind it, the launcher now starts First Screen right after it. Other system tiles keep the error-only fallback. Thanks to pponce for the fix and the test on the S95C (issue #170, pull request #169); the Q70C report came with a diagnostic export. Not tried on a Q70C yet.

### Vetro UI

- **Vetro UI: the launcher, live TV and the on-screen displays on one page.** The Launcher and Vetro TV Tuner entries become one, **Vetro UI**, with four tabs: an **Overview** with one switch per part and how the parts depend on each other, **Launcher** (the Vetro launcher or another app of your choice, the former Custom launcher tab), **Live TV** (the tuner, its TV guide and its other parts, the remote buttons and the previews) and **On-screen displays** (the volume bar with its position and the number pad, with pictures that follow the position). Switching between the Vetro launcher and another app is one button and one question. The volume bar and the 123 pad say when they cannot work (no launcher on the Home key, interception off). [Guide](app/docs/VETRO-UI.md).
- **Vetro UI's keys in the Remapper.** Switching on the volume bar or the 123 pad turns the remote interception on by itself (only then: a user's off stays off) and lists their keys, locked, first among the shortcuts. A shortcut of yours on the same key is marked *Overridden by Vetro UI*; Vetro UI asks before it takes such a key, and the Remapper asks before you save a shortcut on a held key or turn the interception off while Vetro UI needs it. Turning the launcher on or off now also updates the keys the remote bridge holds back.
- **Task manager in the Vetro launcher, by [zabique](https://github.com/zabique) ([#172](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/pull/172)).** Now a card of the Control center (above). Down on the apps row opens a list of the running apps in the dock's glass, with CPU and memory (private RAM plus swap of the app and its web renderers) and the free memory. OK switches to the app, holding OK closes it (a tap only acts when the key comes up, so a click never closes an app), Up or Back returns. Live TV, the on-screen menus, HbbTV, the keyboard, Vetro and two Samsung helpers are left out. **Opt-in:** a Task manager switch on Vetro UI's Launcher tab and Overview, with a preview; off, the launcher starts with `--no-tasks` and looks as before. When on, a “Task manager” hint sits under the bar, 46 px higher. Reduce motion applies to it. Tried by its author on a QN85A; holding OK on a physical remote and a Q80A run are still to do.
- **The 123 pad no longer leaks its keys to the tuner.** With the tuner on and a channel on screen, moving with Left/Right in the launcher's 123 pad also switched the audio track and opened the tuner's channel pad, because the tuner reads the remote straight from the input device. Tested on the computer only.

- **The remote's key pictures everywhere a screen names a key.** The task manager's footer already drew them; the TV guide's footer still spelled the keys ("OK  Watch  Left / Right  Time …") and so did the Quick controls line of the Vetro page on the TV. - **The 123 pad's Info chip opens the tuner's own info panel.** It used to send the Info key by name, which only Samsung's app sees, so nothing appeared at the top. With Vetro's Live TV on screen it now asks the tuner for its panel (`info` on its control pipe), and sends the key by name as before when the tuner is not running or not on screen.
- **One window answers each key of the remote.** A review of how the launcher, the 123 pad, the tuner and the Remapper share the remote (they read it straight from the input device, whatever window has it) found several places where two of them answered the same press. Fixed, tested on the computer and in the preview, not yet on a TV:
  - with the **osd 123** open over the launcher or the Task manager, the launcher no longer moves or opens the focused tile under it; when Samsung's own 123 pad opens (Vetro's is off, or the key came on a part of the remote the bridge does not hold), the launcher steps aside;
  - with the **launcher** open, the tuner no longer answers or wakes up (CH+ brought the tuner up over the launcher, and both then answered the arrows): the Dock and the Task manager leave a flag file like the 123 pad's, now holding the launcher's pid, so a flag left by a launcher that was killed is ignored at once;
  - **Home then Back** no longer leaves Samsung's tuner answering the remote: the launcher tells the tuner when it closes without opening anything (or after picking the TV input), and the tuner comes back if live TV is on screen; after Samsung's Home it comes back when you return to live TV;

  - the **osd 123** sends its keys once its window is gone (a slow fade could swallow typed digits), and its colour and teletext keys send the tuner away first so they reach Samsung's live TV app; its digits go to the tuner only through a real pipe;

### Fixes

- **Port already in use.** If another app on the TV holds Vetro's port (8099 is also where tizen-youtube and TizenTube listen), the service used to end with the error and auto-restart brought it back into the same error, over and over. It now says which port is taken, keeps running and listens as soon as the port is free. The default stays 8099: the widget's access origin lives in the signed package, which an update cannot change. To use another port, build with `VETRO_PORT` (see the installation guide).
- **Leftovers of an uninstall.** `pkgcmd -u` removes the package but not what Samsung's own uninstaller removes: the app's row in the web app registry, its icon folder and its line in the Home row. Measured on a Q80A after removing Disney+ with Vetro: all three stayed, so the Home kept a tile that did not open (a sideloaded TizenTube left two Home lines too). Samsung's command for the proper uninstall, `wascmd`, is switched off on released firmware. Vetro now reads the app's registry ids before the uninstall and, once the package is verified gone, tells the Home as Samsung does (`memory/was/app_uninstall`), removes the Home line, the registry row and the icon folder, keeping a copy of each file or row it changes in `/opt/usr/home/owner/share/vetro-uninstall-backup`. The Home row file is not rewritten while the Home is running (it would write the line back); the answer then says `pending`. **Rebuild the launcher app list** (Launcher tab) now also takes out of the Home row the lines of apps that are known nowhere, left by earlier uninstalls (the same cleanup is available as `POST /home-order {"action":"clean"}`). The cleanup steps were done by hand and checked on one Q80A; the code itself is tested against copies of the files, not on a TV.
- **Launcher after an uninstall.** The launcher's app list is rebuilt right after Vetro uninstalls a package, and an app that the web app registry still lists but the package manager no longer knows gets no tile (it would not open). Not tried on a TV.
- **Samsung Home closed after a boot.** With the launcher on, the TV still started Samsung's own Home at boot and it stayed idle in memory (about 140 MB on a Q80A) until the first Home press or until the switch was turned on again. Vetro now closes it once, when the launcher is running and the TV has been up for five minutes (never earlier: a Q80A without it stalls its boot). It is not closed again, so opening it from the launcher on purpose keeps working. Measured on a Q80A by closing it by hand: it did not come back, Home went to the launcher and about 130 MB were freed; the automatic close itself is tested, not tried across a reboot.
- **Samsung TV Plus switch** in Quick settings. TV Plus is one reversible group (the app, its deep-link and OTA helpers, `slive-daemon` and `bms-service`); because the two services are systemd units that restart themselves, the switch also puts Samsung's own do-not-start files in place and stops them. Turning it off frees about 25 MB on a Q80A (used memory 950 to 928 MB, measured). The launcher and the dock also stop Samsung's preview updater (it only feeds the Home tiles, about 13 MB) and start it again when they go off.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-06

Pairing for the dashboard and API, community plugins (Vetro Shards), a custom launcher, more privacy switches, signed in-place updates and a safer Restore the TV. There was no 0.4.0 release: its work is included here.

### Sign-in, plugins and updates

- **Pairing for the dashboard and API** ([#5](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/5) requested by **PythEch**; [#9](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/pull/9) by **zerodevb**). Browsers and scripts pair once with a six-digit code shown on the TV. Up to 20 devices can be paired. The TV app keeps working without pairing. [Sign-in guide](app/docs/SIGN-IN.md).
- **Pairing can be turned off.** Quick settings → Developer settings → Pairing allows dashboard and API access without a code. It is off by default, asks for confirmation and shows a warning while access is open. Restore the TV turns pairing back on.
- **Vetro Shards (plugins), experimental.** Install, update and remove `.vplugin` bundles from a public catalog or a file. New plugins start switched off, and a plugin that keeps failing is stopped. If Vetro was stopped right after starting, plugins start in safe mode and the page says why; nothing is removed. Plugin pages open as tabs. Plugin code has the same access as Vetro, including root commands: permission labels are not a security sandbox. [Guide and developer tools](app/docs/plugins.md).
- **Signed plugin catalog.** Catalog plugins are checked against a signature, and invalid ones are refused. Unsigned plugins need **Developer mode**, which is off by default. Each plugin shows where it came from. A signature shows who published a plugin, not that it is safe. Plugins with accented characters now install on older TVs.
- **The launcher is the first bundled plugin.** It updates with Vetro and cannot be removed or replaced from the catalog. [Architecture](app/docs/PLUGIN-ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Signed in-place updates, launcher included.** Software update checks GitHub when you ask, verifies the bundle, replaces the files and restarts the launcher if needed. If the new version does not start, Vetro goes back to the previous one within four minutes; you can also roll back by hand. A second update is refused while the first one waits for confirmation ([#116](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/116), reported by **adri0894-web**). Changes to `config.xml` still need a reinstall. Tested on a Q80A with the release key: update, refusal of a tampered bundle, rollback by hand and automatic rollback. [Update guide](app/docs/UPDATING.md).

### Launcher and remote

- **Custom launcher: your own app on the Home key.** The Launcher page has two tabs, **Vetro launcher** and **Custom launcher**. Pick an installed app and turn the switch on: the Home key opens that app. Samsung's Home stays enabled, Vetro closes it after each Home press, and Autorun Smart Hub is switched off so the TV does not power on into it. Turning the switch off undoes all of it. If the app keeps failing to start, the mode turns itself off and the Home key goes back to Samsung. Keep Samsung's Home enabled: on the Q80A, disabling it can keep the TV from finishing its boot. The separate **Samsung Home** switch is now at the bottom of the Custom launcher tab and asks for confirmation. Not yet tried by hand on a TV. Related request: [#127](https://github.com/andrearicci93/vetro/issues/127) by **skjnldsv** (an app on the TV's home screen at power-on); this mode does not cover it.

Better key handling, and Samsung's Home panels that overlap the launcher are closed. **Reduce motion** turns off all launcher animations. Choosing Samsung Home right after pressing Home no longer closes it again.

Two switches in the Launcher tab, both off by default. *HDMI sources on Home* shows the HDMI tiles already in Samsung's Home row, in the same positions. *HDMI source images* uses Samsung's artwork instead of generic icons. On TVs where the Home row holds only recent apps, the launcher adds the other visible installed apps. Tested on a Q80A, where nothing changes; not tested on a 2022 TV.

While remote shortcuts are active, keys that keep their normal action now work in Tizen's on-screen keyboard. Tested on a Q80A.

Opening Samsung's AgingApp could leave the TV starting a factory colour test at every boot, with the volume bar hidden, even after a factory reset. The Home-picture host `cdn-stv.adgrx.com` (#79 by **RyanDChavez**; #109) stays out of the default lists: blocking it alone does not remove pictures already cached.

A request or plugin timer that fails no longer stops the whole service. DNS blocking is restored even if root starts late. Saved remaps, sign-in sessions and launcher lists are written so a power cut is much less likely to corrupt them. A failed remote action pauses shortcuts for a minute instead of for good. Launcher: Home keys seen by the display server are handled, the first frame after reopening is current, and a held key no longer reaches the reopened launcher. Dashboard: the Privacy panel shows reported consents, conflicting shortcut saves are refused, leaving the Remapper ends listening, a request the TV never answers is cancelled after two minutes instead of locking a switch, unsaved domain text is no longer overwritten, and a plugin page stops running when you leave it.
