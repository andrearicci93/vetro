# Vetro design system

The single record of how Vetro looks. Open `index.html` in a browser to see it; `tokens.css` holds the values.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `tokens.css` | Every colour, size, radius and duration, as `--vetro-*` custom properties. Dark by default; `data-vetro-theme="light"` on a container switches to light. Reduced motion sets both durations to 0. The `--vetro-tv-*` block holds the values of the native TV screens (dock, tuner, guide). |
| `index.html` | A reference page that renders the palette, gradients, both themes and the rhythm from `tokens.css`, then the TV material and **every TV screen as a mock-up** (Dock, Task manager, Volume bar, osd 123, Banner, Info, Channel list, Channel pad, Audio toast, EPG) built from the `--vetro-tv-*` tokens and the pictures in `assets/` (the Control center's cards, All apps and the move mode are in `dock.html`), with sliders to try values and a switch for the backdrop (video, black, light, checker). |
| `dock.html`, `tuner.html`, `epg.html` | The three TV interfaces **as they are on the TV**, full screen and driven by the keyboard like the remote (arrows, Enter, Esc, PageUp / PageDown for CH+ / CH-, digits in the tuner). They draw with the real pictures in `assets/` (`dock/res`, `tuner/res`: veil, panel and rim, focus capsule, progress, ring, glow, system icons) and port the layout code: `DockLayout` and `Design.cs` (dock, linear 0.30 s motion, entrance stagger, glow cross-fade, Sources on Up, Task manager on Down, Volume bar on + / -, osd 123 on N), the tuner's Banner, Info, Channel list, Channel pad and Audio toast with their timers (Banner 4 s, Info 10 s, list 8 s, pad 20 s, number taken after 2 s, Audio toast 2.2 s), and `GuideGrid` / `GuideScene` (180-minute window, 72 h horizon, row scrolling, background reading of the schedules). `?still` skips the dock entrance and every auto-hide timer (for screenshots); `dock.html?view=sources|tasks|volume|pad123`, `tuner.html?view=info|list|pad|toast|subtitles` and `epg.html?ch=N` open a state directly. Shared: `tv-common.css` (page shell), `tv-material.css` (SamsungOneUI from `assets/fonts/`, the veil, panel, rim, focus capsule, progress and EPG cells, drawn with the real white pictures tinted through a CSS mask; also linked by `index.html`), `tv-common.js` (helpers: `Tv.panel`, `Tv.cap`, `Tv.veil`, `Tv.progressRow`…; `?bd=` picks the backdrop on the three full-screen pages). |
| `assets/` | Copies of `dock/res` and `tuner/res` (plus `assets/fonts/`). The opus6 pictures: `tuner/veil_corner.png`, `progress.png`, `bar.png`, `pill.png`, `hatch.png`, `hatch_ends.png`, `pill_bg.png`, `pill_rim.png`, `pill_cap.png`; `dock/pill_*.png`, `vol_*.png`. `dock_bg`, `shadow`, `solid`, `ring` stay for the retired sheet. |
| `icon-reference.png` | The app icon the palette was sampled from (the same picture as `app/icon.png`). |

## Where it is used

- **Dashboard** (`app/service/dashboard.js`): the token block is copied into the page's `:root`, then short names (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--border`, `--accent`…) point at it. `app/tests/dashboard-v2.test.cjs` fails if the page and `tokens.css` drift apart, so change `tokens.css` first and then the page.
- **Icon**: `app/icon.png` is the app icon. The service serves it at `/icon.png`; the dashboard shows it in the sidebar and as the page icon.
- **Launcher** (`dock/`): it has its own look, chosen in `dock/designer` and recorded in `dock/designer/chosen.json`. `dock/src/Core/Design.cs` is the single source for the native code. Its colours follow the icons it shows, not these tokens.
- **App icons in the launcher** (`dock/src/Core/IconPrep.cs`): a square icon fills its tile; an opaque icon that is not square (a banner) fills the tile too, cut to its middle square (never letterboxed: the blurred bands came out as noise on the TV); a logo on transparency sits smaller, whole, on a dark glass tile. Corners rounded and the thin rim on all three.

## Rules

- About 80% of the interface stays neutral (`--vetro-bg`, `--vetro-surface`, `--vetro-border`). Blue marks actions and selections.
- Actions use Cobalto (`--vetro-action`) with light text; Luce (`--vetro-blue-400`) is for details and the progress bar. Links and focus use `--vetro-link`.
- Controls are 44 px high with an 8 px radius; surfaces have a 16 px radius. Focus is a 2 px ring, 3 px away.
- Glass and gradients are for small overlays and brand moments only. Everyday surfaces are solid.
- **Never a coloured top border on a rounded card**: a thicker line on top makes coloured "ears" at the corners. Give a card a theme colour with a faint background, a uniform border and a coloured label or icon.
- A switch is a **win**: its title says what you get ("Disable HbbTV", "Block firmware and software updates"), and *on* means the win is applied. A chip under the title says *Done* or *Partly done*, in plain words. A switch that may not work on every TV, or that is new and still being tried, sits in Quick settings' **Experimental** tab (since 0.7.1), whose note says so once; its card carries no Experimental chip of its own. A whole page that may not work on every TV (Vetro UI) carries it beside the page title, as a neutral chip ("Experimental · some parts may not work on your TV"): a permanent caveat is never amber.
- A win that needs root has **no switch without root**: the chip says *Root required*, and the card says what still works instead of offering a switch that would fail (the firmware card lists the network steps: no internet for the TV over IPv4 and IPv6, a VLAN, an allowlist, logging; see `app/docs/UPDATES-WITHOUT-ROOT.md`).
- **A switch shows only where it can act** (since 0.7.1 written down): a group whose apps the TV does not have, AirPlay without its receiver, and the mask switches (Samsung remote support, Background app delivery, Wi-Fi presence) without their services are left out, not shown as "Not available". A group's *Partly done* chip names each gap in the same sentence: components still enabled, components that still start at boot, and Samsung's own "do not start" files of its background services ("2 background services can still start" while the group is off, "… are still kept from starting" while it is on).
- **A key that closes an overlay acts on its release** (since 0.7.1, #284): Back and Exit close the Dock, the Task manager and the osd 123 when the key comes up, so the app under them never gets the rest of the key (YouTube left the video it was playing). A new overlay that closes on Back follows the same rule.
- **Keys of the remote are pictures, not words.** Wherever a screen tells you to press a key (the task manager's and the TV guide's footers, the hint under All apps and Sources, the Audio toast's two rows, the Quick controls line of the Vetro page), it draws the key: OK, the four pad directions, the CH rocker, Back, from `app/design-system/remote`. The native screens use PNGs of them (`assets/tuner/key_*.png`, `assets/dock/key_*.png`). Prose that mentions a key stays words.
- **The 123 pad's palette key** is a white outline (`assets/dock/pad_colors.png`, tinted with the cell's ink) with its four dots in a second picture (`pad_colors_dots.png`) that keeps its colours: red, green, yellow, blue along the arc from the lower left over the top to the right, the colours and order of the colour keys.
- **Channel logos (tuner).** The tuner shows the logo Samsung's own tuner shows (the TV's saved picture, round, 87 px source) wherever a channel is named and there is room: Banner (72 px at the right end of the block, x 864, the name narrowed to make room), Channel list (44 px between number and name), Channel pad (44 px before the name of the typed channel), Info (72 px at the right end of the block, x 1224) and EPG (56 px in the label, the number over the name). Sizes are `--vetro-tv-logo-*` in `tokens.css`. The logo is a picture on its own round plate, drawn straight on the panel or the veil: no extra frame, no tint. **A channel with no logo keeps the column empty**, so names line up; with no data at all (switch `logos` off, tables unreadable) every view draws as it did before logos. Logos come only from the TV (matched by service id or DVB triplet, never by name) and are **not** copied into `assets/` or `tuner/res`: the mock-ups use a plain stand-in disc with the initials. A wrong logo is worse than none.
- **Every graphic asset used by the TV UI lives in `assets/` too**: a picture added to `dock/res` or `tuner/res` is copied here, and the mock-ups use these files (see AGENTS.local.md).
- Copy is English, short, and says what changes on the TV. A feature that is not finished carries a **Beta** chip. A whole area that is new and still being shaped carries a **Preview** chip in the sidebar (`chip nav-count`): Vetro UI in 0.7.0.

## Page rules (the dashboard)

The dashboard's pages follow seven rules, written in full in `app/design-system/DESIGN.md` ("Page rules") and first applied to Vetro Shards on 2026-11-17: one title and one sentence; how-to text only in an empty state; one colour per meaning (amber only for what needs attention now, never for a permanent caveat); at most one main action in a view; an error sits where the content would be, message first and retry on the same row; one name per thing; a card is for a list or a group, and an empty list is one sentence. The same file's "Layout" says how density is kept (settings in two columns where the container allows, a preview beside its settings, one short sentence per setting).

Vetro UI → Overview: the pictures and "How they fit together" on the left; on the right two cards, **Launcher + Control center** (what the Home key opens) and **Live TV + Volume bar + Number pad**. The TV's own Vetro page has **four** quick-control tiles (Vetro launcher, Disable ads and viewing data, Disable all voice assistants, Update Vetro): the Vetro UI tile was taken out on 2026-11-20, its parts are switched in the dashboard.

Vetro UI → On-screen displays, under the Volume bar's position: one muted sentence on where the TV's sound goes and what Vetro's bar does there (from the launcher status's `audioOutput`: Samsung's speaker selection, `ext_volume`, whether `mbr-daemon` runs). TV speakers say nothing. A receiver on HDMI, the optical outputs (optical, TV + optical, centre modes) and Bluetooth say that Vol and Mute pass to Samsung and Samsung's bar shows; Wi-Fi speakers and the rest say the TV's own level changes and that this has not been tried. A permanent caveat, so muted text, never a box.

Two tabs of Quick settings must never be confused, and both carry the same pair of lines: **Disable** switches an app off and leaves it on the TV (one switch brings it back); **Uninstall** deletes it from the TV (space freed, not undone from here). Uninstall lists only what the service says this TV has and allows to remove, each with its icon and size, and asks for a confirmation in the row before anything is sent.

## Names of the TV screens

- **Dock**: the bar of apps (Samsung's Home row as the base, then the user's own order: hold OK on a tile to move it or take it out). **All apps**: the Dock's page of every app (its pill at the end of the row), by name, ending with the Settings pill and the store pill (TV-Store when it is installed, Samsung's App store otherwise) (hold OK adds an app to the row or takes it out; on **Sources**, the page of inputs, hold OK does the same for TV, HDMI 1-4 and, on a monitor that has one, DisplayPort, last, with its own `in-dp` picture). **Control center**: Down on the dock, up to three cards side by side. A card shows **five rows at most**; side by side the cards always stand five rows tall, whatever their lists hold, so nothing grows or shrinks while the Control center is open (a single card is as tall as its own list, 2 to 5 rows). A longer list scrolls with the selection and shows a **scroll bar**: a 4 px capsule (`pill_cap`, ends round) centred in the 16 px gutter right of the rows, from the first plate's top to the last one's bottom; the track white at .14, the thumb ice at .6 (.3 on a card without the focus), as tall as the share of the list on screen and never under 28 px. **Task manager**: its card of running apps, biggest first; the order is set when the card opens (and again after a close) and does not move on its own while it is open; **Audio output**: its card of sound outputs (an external output that plays, such as Optical or a receiver on HDMI, gets its own ticked row with the speaker glyph `vol_icon_2`; the TV speakers are ticked only when they play; once the sound goes to the TV speakers or a Bluetooth device, the external output last seen playing keeps its row, unticked, right under the TV speakers, and choosing it sends the sound back there; Wi-Fi speakers, Sound Share, Auracast and headphone outputs are not offered again), ending with **Turn off screen** (an action, not an output: drawn with the television glyph `out_tv` because 0.7.0 cannot carry one more new file to TVs updating from 0.5.0 (the update's apply script is at 99.9% of 64 KiB); an eye-slash glyph is the intended picture once 0.7.0 is the base; never ticked; the card closes and the screen goes dark while the sound stays, Samsung's Picture Off; any key but volume and power brings the picture back). **Shard card**: a card a Shard (a third-party plugin) adds to the Control center, titled with the Shard's label. **Volume bar**: the pill that shows the level. **osd 123**: the launcher's 123 bar (digits, Done, the palette to the colour keys).
- Tuner: **Banner** (the box that appears when the channel changes), **Channel list** (OK), **Channel pad** (the tuner's own number entry, Right), **Info** (the Banner's corner grown up and to the right, on the Info key), **EPG** (the TV guide), **Audio toast** (the audio track and the subtitles, Left then Down).

## Two surfaces, one language

| | Web (dashboard) | TV (dock, tuner, guide) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drawn with | HTML/CSS | EFL (Evas) from C#, in a transparent window over the live picture |
| Colour | blue marks actions; neutrals everywhere else | **no accent**: the veil or the slate panel, white ink, hierarchy by opacity; Luce only for progress; colour only from app icons and logos |
| Focus | 2 px ring, 3 px away | the ice capsule with slate text; on the dock icons a 2 px ice ring and a glow in the icon's own colour |
| Depth | `--vetro-shadow`, glass only for small overlays | **no drop shadow**: a translucent panel with a 2 px light rim, or the veil; no blur of the picture behind |
| Tokens | `--vetro-*` | `--vetro-tv-*` (dark only, canvas pixels at 1920x1080) |

## TV material (native screens)

Approved on 2026-11-11 (design round opus6: `challenge/opus6/NOTE.md` and its PNGs). **Ink.** White at 100 / 70 / 55% on the panel (main / muted / faint); on the veil 100 / 76 / 64%, the Banner's programme at 92%. Slate only on the ice focus. Luce (#A5D8FF) only for progress, the volume level, the EPG's now line and a switch that is on (the Vetro page on the TV).

**Type.** SamsungOneUI in three weights, never synthetic bold: **200** for the tuner's big numbers and the clock (Banner number 128, Info number 64, Info clock 88), **500** for text, **600** for names and titles. Sizes: 22 caption, 24 label, 26 meta, 28 body, 30 subtitle, 32 row, 40 key, 56 Banner name, 64 EPG title, 72 Info title. Text is cut with an ellipsis, never wrapped, except the EPG's two and the Info's two description lines. **Ask for a weight by one family name** ("SamsungOneUI 600"), never with a fallback list ("SamsungOneUI 600,Sans"): Evas gives the whole string to fontconfig as one family, which does not exist, and the TV then draws every weight as 400 (seen on the Q80A, 2026-11-11). **Place a text by its baseline with Evas's metrics**: Evas puts the baseline at y + max ascent, taken from the font's bounding box (SamsungOneUI head.yMax 1047/1024), not hhea (952), and a text with the soft shadow starts 1 px right of and below its object. The tuner's `Core/TextMetrics.cs` does this; the preview draws texts the same way. Measured: the Banner, Info and EPG baselines on the TV are within 1 px of the mock-up.

**Motion.** Launcher: 300 ms at constant speed, 24 ms between one icon and the next; Control center cards 140 ms (the selection; Left / Right moves the focus at once), osd 123 120 ms. Tuner (coded in the opus6 fork, `tuner/src/Core/Motion.cs`): the veil grows Banner -> Info -> EPG in 320 ms while number, name and title fly to their new place (an `evas_map` uniform zoom of the larger copy; Banner -> EPG cross-fades the number, weight 200 -> 500, in the last 80 ms), focus and zap 220 ms, enter and exit 200 ms. Only position, size, alpha and a uniform map move. The "ease" is three linear segments (60% of the way in the first third, 90% in the second): piecewise linear, never a curve evaluated per frame. `--no-motion` (or the `motion` feature off) makes everything immediate.

**Objects per window** (opus6 count, clip included): Dock 25, Sources 14 (24 once an input is in the row: a tick disc and its check for each input), Task manager card 19 + 4 per app (5 when it has no icon), Audio output card 16 + 3 per row (every card has the scroll bar's two capsules, transparent until its list is longer than five rows), Channel pad 31, Channel list 37, osd 123 21, Volume 8, Audio toast 9 (rim, body, 2 keys, 2 labels, 2 values, clip), one-line message 4, Banner 11, Info 16-17, EPG 114 (a "no information" cell is three objects: two ends and the middle).

**Open (from the NOTE, check on the Q80A).** The halo is the classic soft-shadow text style, which the catalogue below lists "with care" (10-16 ms for 12 copies of 4-8 labels): the Banner has 7-9 such labels, the Info up to 18 (two description lines and the three parts of the Next line since 2026-11-17), and they have not been timed on the Q80A yet; the cheaper text filter program (2.2 ms) is the fallback. Banding in the veil's long tail is judged on a photo of the panel. The osd 123's typed number was hard to read on snow (68 px, outside the capsule, no shadow): it is now 36 px on a `pill_chip` (done).

## Performance first

The TV is not a browser: a design that looks free on the web can cost frames there. Measured on the Q80A (firmware 2310.0, 145 prototypes, 2026-11-07; full data in `graphic-testing/report/`, hand-written conclusions in `report/FINDINGS.md`). The panel loop is capped at 20 ms (50 fps), so "free" means "no cost visible under the cap".

Rules every TV screen follows:

1. **Bake once, move forever.** Veils, glows, gradients, borders are pictures made at start-up (or PNGs prebaked on the Mac: `veil_corner`, `pill_bg`, `pill_rim`, `pill_cap`, `progress`, `pill`, `hatch`, `hatch_ends`). On screen they are only moved, resized, faded or mapped.
2. **No pixel work per frame.** No re-blur, regenerated gradient or recomputed shadow, not even an image upload inside an animation. Animate position, size, opacity and `evas_map` only.
3. **Translucency and render ops are free.** Alpha cards, ADD/MUL accents, rotations, zoom, 3D and per-vertex alpha cost nothing visible at 12 copies.
4. **The budget is objects, not effects.** Stay under about 200 live objects; 300 moving rectangles or 200 labels already take ~19 ms of CPU per 20 ms frame. The guide has ~400 objects (140 fills, 260 texts): static it is fine, in motion it is the screen to measure.
5. **Glass is tint + hairline + top highlight.** Blur of what is behind a client window is not available; bake a blur only from pictures we draw ourselves.
6. **Draw our own components.** `elm_toolbar` takes 1.6 s to create; the component sets (buttons, rows, grids) take 12-48 ms and nothing per frame.
7. **No object filter programs** (`efl_gfx_filter_program_set`) until investigated: every one crashed the app in the test. Text filter programs (shadow, glow, outline on a label) work and are fine.
8. **Baking is start-up or idle work** (40-150 ms per shadow or glass), never on a focus change.

### Element catalogue

Process CPU is per 20 ms frame with 12 copies of the element on screen (about a quarter of the screen); "build" is the one-off cost to create one. Figures: `graphic-testing/report/REPORT.md`.

**Use: no visible cost (50 fps, CPU 2-5 ms)**

| Element | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Baked 9-slice panels and capsules (`pill_bg`, `pill_rim`, `pill_cap`, `pill` PNG, tinted by colour; radius by `border_scale`) | The base of every surface. Resize and fade freely. |
| Baked shadows, any radius 8-64, two-layer elevation, coloured glow | CPU 2-3 ms; build 38 / 50 / 73 / 147 ms for blur 8 / 16 / 32 / 64, 128 ms for two layers. Bake once per size, reuse. |
| Baked glass card: tint + hairline + top highlight | CPU 4-5 ms; build 112-150 ms. Sharp picture shows through: that is the look. |
| Frosted card over **our own** backdrop: a low-resolution copy scaled up with smoothing (`L10`) | The only blur seen in the tests. CPU 2.7 ms, build 38 ms. Never over live video. |
| Opacity, colour and group-alpha changes, fades, sliding panels | Free in motion. |
| `evas_map`: rotate, zoom, 3D with perspective and lighting, per-vertex alpha, UV mirror | Carousel and spinning card in motion: CPU 4 ms. Set the map once, then move it. |
| Render ops ADD, SUB, MUL, TINT and the COPY "hole" | CPU 4-9 ms. |
| Static gradients (baked) | CPU 2-3 ms. Check dark banding in the shots before choosing the stops. |
| Plain text, SamsungOneUI 200-700, 12-72 px, colour hierarchy by alpha | CPU 3-4 ms. |
| Text filter programs (`evas_object_text_filter_program_set`): shadow, glow, outline | Render correctly; CPU 2.2 ms. The cheapest way to make a label legible on video. |
| Full-screen image or alpha layers; a 1080p JPEG | CPU 1-3 ms. |
| Elementary button, check, radio, slider, progressbar, label/entry, spinner, list, segment, diskselector, popup | Fine at run time. |

**With care: costs something, spend it deliberately**

| Element | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Classic text styles (shadow, outline, glow, soft shadow) | CPU 10-16 ms for 12 copies of 4-8 labels vs ~3.5 ms plain. A few labels on video, not a whole list. The Banner (7-9) and Info (up to 16) use the soft shadow as their halo: to time on the Q80A. |
| Object count | 300 moving rectangles, 200 labels or many small images: CPU 19-20 ms of a 20 ms frame. Budget ~200 live objects. |
| Re-setting an `evas_map` every frame | CPU 10.6 ms vs 3-4 ms for a map set once and moved. |
| Baking (any of the 40-150 ms items above) | Start-up or idle only, never on a focus change; never 12 times when one image can be shared. |
| Component sets drawn by hand (buttons, rows, tile grid) | 12-48 ms to create, nothing per frame. |

**Avoid: measured as a problem, or not proven**

| Element | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Any pixel work per frame: re-blurred glass, recomputed shadow, rewritten gradient | 1 fps (114 ms a copy), 2 fps (40 ms), 3 fps (31 ms) at 12 copies. |
| Uploading a finished image every frame (`image_data_copy_set` in an animation) | 21 fps at 12 copies (2.3 ms each). |
| `elm_toolbar` | 1.6 s to create, ~110 theme errors. |
| Object filter programs (`efl_gfx_filter_program_set`: blur, curve, grow, tint, filter shadows) | Crashed the app in every test. |
| Legacy snapshot and proxy + filter as "blur what is behind" | No blur seen; a snapshot only sees our own canvas, never the video. |
| Blurring the live video | No client API found (`elm_win_blur_set` is not exported). Do not promise it. |
| EO snapshot, webp, svg | Not verified (a test bug and unresolved loaders): do not rely on them yet. |

Costs at 12 copies: baked images, maps, render ops, text styles, solid/glass/outline component sets, common Elementary widgets: 50 fps. CPU glass re-blurred each frame: 1 fps (114 ms a copy). Shadow per frame: 2 fps (40 ms). Animated gradient: 3 fps (31 ms). Per-frame upload of a finished image: 21 fps (2.3 ms).

## Iterating

- Open `index.html`, switch the backdrop to **Video** and to **Light**: the veil and the panels sit in front of the live picture on the TV, and a light picture is the hard case for the veil. Use the sliders for the veil alphas, the panel colour, opacity, radius and rim, the focus, the EPG cells, muted text and icon glow; **Copy changed tokens** gives a `:root { ... }` block to paste into `tokens.css`.
- For a faithful look at one TV interface, open `dock.html`, `tuner.html` or `epg.html`; the sample data (apps, channels, programmes) is at the top of each script. The dock's app icons are stand-ins (the TV reads the real Home row), the tuner and guide data are generated.
- To change a screen, edit its function in the script at the bottom of `index.html` (`screens.dock`, `.tasks`, `.volume`, `.pad123`, `.banner`, `.info`, `.list`, `.pad`, `.toast`, `.guide`); to add one (Vetro Home, PIN, plugins), add a function, a `<div class="tv-canvas" id="s-NAME">` and a heading.
- The mock-ups are not pixel-exact: they load SamsungOneUI from `assets/fonts/` (200, 500, 600), but the browser's text rendering and the halo (CSS `text-shadow` for the EVAS soft shadow) differ from the TV's. On the three full-screen pages the panel, rim and focus are the real white pictures tinted through a CSS mask; `index.html` shows them scaled to half and draws the same shapes with `border-radius` (the masks leave seams when scaled). For the exact opus6 compositions and transitions, open `challenge/opus6/index.html`. Judge proportions, hierarchy and opacity here; judge the final look on the TV.
- Check performance on the TV with `graphic-testing` before adding any new effect: a tile, `session.py bench --only <id>`, the verdict is in `REPORT.md`.
