A cleaner Home for your Samsung TV

Vetro is an open source launcher, debloater and remapper for rooted Samsung Tizen TVs. Replace Home, disable selected tracking services and give remote buttons new shortcuts.

Install VetroHow Samsung TV rooting works

Vetro 0.5.0. Root features require a compatible Tizen 6+ TV. Experimental software; launcher in beta.

Vetro runs on the TV. Open its dashboard from a computer or phone on the same network.

Check your Samsung TV first

Vetro is developed and tested on the Q80A. Other models have community reports, with different results by firmware. Root features target Tizen 6 and newer; Tizen 9 and 10 have mixed reports. Older TVs have limited dashboard support.

Replace Samsung Home

Press Home for a minimal app bar with the same apps and icons in the same order. Samsung Home closes while Vetro's launcher is on, and remains one press away. The launcher returns after a restart or standby.

On one Q80A measurement, available RAM rose from 533 MB to 662 MB. This measures memory, not frame rate; other TVs will differ.

Read about the Samsung TV launcher

Choose what runs on your TV

Quick settings groups privacy and background-service switches. Task manager explains apps and services so you can disable selected components. Disabling keeps a component installed and can be reversed; uninstalling needs an installer to recover.

DNS blocking does not guarantee that every app's ads or telemetry are blocked.

Give remote buttons new shortcuts

Press a remote button, choose an app and save the shortcut. Saved shortcuts survive restarts. The Home Assistant page can also turn a key press into an MQTT message.

See Samsung remote remapping

Install Vetro on a compatible TV

Apps2Samsung handles signing and installation. After installing, open Vetro once, set Host PC IP to 127.0.0.1 in Developer Mode and restart the TV. Follow the complete guide for prerequisites and checks.

Follow the Vetro installation guideRead the source and release notes

The dashboard uses plain HTTP. Keep it on a trusted local network and never expose it to the Internet. Automatic pairing starts after the TV app has been seen; until then the dashboard can be open.
